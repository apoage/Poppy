### 9. Diagnosis — Why the Head Didn't Propagate

The auxiliary loss shapes the *latent* but has no structural path to the *policy head*. 0.05 × 0.68 = 0.034 of direction pressure vs BC + policy losses competing for the same latent. Three compounding issues:

1. **Latent ceiling**: `dir_acc` stalling at 0.553 (close to random-plus-epsilon) across 300 epochs suggests the encoder latent has limited per-asset direction capacity. Audit's cosine 0.38 was measured at macro-regime granularity (pure bull vs pure bear). Per-asset direction in a mixed window is much harder.
2. **No propagation path**: policy head reads latent, gets weak direction signal, can't extract it. Classic bottleneck.
3. **`bc_max_trade_weight=4`** (dialed down from 64) removed TASK_726's uniform-collapse but also reduced the trade-gradient magnitude. Policy settled at "mostly HOLD, symmetric noise" — worst of both worlds.

### 13. TASK_728b — Refiner Warm-Start Patch

### 18. Root Cause — Encoder Bottleneck

Connecting the last three runs:
- TASK_727: dir_acc plateau at 0.55 → encoder couldn't carry per-asset direction.
- TASK_728 (encoder unfreeze): dir_acc → 0.60. Encoder grew some direction capacity.
- TASK_728c (sharper target + trained refiner): policy loss descends, but Bal.Acc stays at 34%.

The pattern: **Bal.Acc is bounded by dir_acc.** If the latent can only predict per-asset direction at 59%, no policy head can translate that to better than ~34% per-asset action accuracy (5 actions, direction gives you roughly which 2 of 5 are plausible). The 59% ceiling is the encoder's actual capacity for compressing `64 assets × per-asset signal` into a 128-dim global latent.

To break through 40% Bal.Acc, we need to raise dir_acc *itself* past 0.65+. That requires either:

1. **TASK_729a — Per-asset latent routing**: don't force direction through the 64→128 global bottleneck. Extract per-asset encoder features (before the cross-asset pooling) and route them directly to a per-asset direction head and policy head. This is the principled fix.
2. **TASK_729b — Bigger encoder**: wider `embed_dim`, more heads, larger final projection. More brute-force, less elegant.
3. **TASK_729c — Richer inputs**: already audited — 10+ channels carry direction; not the bottleneck.

### Key Lesson

Class-balancing alone is **necessary but not sufficient** when the labels themselves are sparse (4-of-64) and the minority-class gradient lacks direction-specificity. Rebalancing amplifies whatever signal exists; if the signal is weak, amplification produces noise, not correctness. The direction head provides 64× denser per-asset supervision to fix this.
