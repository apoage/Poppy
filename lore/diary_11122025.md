# Diary Entry - 2025-12-11

## Critical Bug Fixes (Financial Logic)

### 1. "Unit Confusion" & The -7713% Bug
- **Issue:** A replay of yesterday's logs revealed a catastrophic trade (ETHBTC) that sized a position at $1.4 Million on a $100 account.
- **Root Cause:** Old logic treated the Quote Currency Price (0.035 BTC) as USD, leading to massive oversizing.
- **Fix:** `portfolio.py` now correctly converts all values using live FX rates before sizing and accounting.
- **Verification:** New paper trading sessions show realistic PnL (-0.36%).

### 2. Spot Mode & Negative Cash
- **Issue:** Paper trader accumulated negative cash ($-37) because it kept buying without selling.
- **Root Cause:** `execute_actions` calculated size based on *Equity* (`total_value`) rather than *Liquidity* (`cash`).
- **Fix:** Implemented strict cash clamping in `portfolio.py`. Buy orders are now capped at `max_spendable_cash`.

### 3. "No Sell Signal" / Masking Bug
- **Issue:** In Spot Mode, the model never issued a Sell command, even for assets it held.
- **Root Cause:** `get_valid_actions` (which generates the mask) was called *before* `update_prices`. Since the portfolio didn't know current prices, it defaulted to assuming "No Holdings" or invalid state, blocking Sells.
- **Fix:** Updated `paper_trade_jax.py` to update portfolio state *immediately* after receiving market data, ensuring the mask is accurate.
