### Training Agreement vs Benchmark — Why 90% ≠ 41%

Training shows ~90% oracle_agreement but benchmark shows ~41% balanced accuracy. Two causes:

1. **HOLD dominance inflates training agreement**: `oracle_hold_ratio=0.96` means 96% of actions are HOLD. Both model and oracle say HOLD most of the time → high raw agreement. But balanced accuracy weights BUY/SELL/HOLD equally, and model is terrible at trade actions on unseen data.

2. **OHLCV channel mismatch**: Model trained on close-only pipeline. After TASK_703, benchmark feeds real OHLCV. 11 of 27 encoder channels (H, L, V, volume ratios, VWAP) now carry real signal where model learned on constants/fakes.

Training `agree_trade=0.847` (85% on trade actions) measures on the training distribution. This doesn't generalize to benchmark periods — different assets, different market conditions, plus the OHLCV input mismatch.

### Launch Issues + Fixes

1. **`--single` ignored `--branch-a-historical`**: `launch_single_branch()` hardcoded Bella. Fixed: new code path in e-dojo dispatch when both `--single` and `--branch-a-historical` set.
2. **Epochs too low**: `--epochs 2500` but resuming from epoch 4324 → empty loop. Fix: use `--epochs 10000`, benchmark auto-stop handles termination.
3. **OOM on 3090**: `vram_fraction=0.45` (shared GPU default) limited to ~10.8GB. Fixed: single historical branch now sets `vram_fraction=0.90`, `gpu_id=0` (3090).
4. **Val MSE = NaN**: Zero-price gaps in historical data → `log(0)` = `-inf` → NaN cascading through cumulative_pnl → remaining_pnl → value MSE. Fixed: clamp both numerator and denominator in log return computation (`jnp.maximum(..., 1e-8)` on both sides).
