realistic memory pressure is worth more than a 5-second smoke that
proves the math works.

mechanism never got out of the gate. Verdict: **OOM-replanned**,

## §127 — TASK_746 value_oracle 5-seed: the strongest signal of the campaign

Variant absolute α: s7 +3.16, s13 +5.21, s23 +7.65, s31 +2.33,
s42 +6.77 → mean **+5.02%**, STD **2.28pp**, **all 5 positive**.
Δα vs proxy baseline (3-seed mean +2.80%) = **+2.23pp**.

The headline number doesn't clear the +3pp promotion bar. The
honest read is: this is **real but small**. But the *shape* of
the signal is qualitatively different from everything else this
campaign has produced:

- **All-positive 5-seed.** First time on Stage-B. 734 had 3/3
  positive at 3 seeds and reversed (s42 −12.71). 746 stayed
  positive at extension. Different distribution.
- **STD 2.28pp** is below the documented single-seed noise floor
  (~5pp). Random-seed noise wouldn't easily produce 5/5 positive
  with this dispersion.
- **Per-period all positive**, including bear and crash — no
  regime damage.
- **Training loss DOWN, Alpha UP.** This is the *planning-side*
  improvement signature we've been hunting since the 737/741/738
  classifier-Alpha anti-correlation pattern crystallized. The
  mechanism is value-target reformulation, not encoder/classifier
  sharpening — and it doesn't show the over-trading collapse.

**Pairing caveat**: s31/s42 ran variant-only. The +2.23pp is a
proxy delta against the 3-seed baseline mean. True paired Δα at
s31/s42 may shift ±5pp per seed. Variant absolute α is robust to
this; the proxy delta is the soft number. **Action**: run paired
a1b1 baselines at s31/s42 — `experiments/752_value_oracle_baseline_pair/`
already speced. Cheap (~6h sequential on one GPU), reusable.

The decision: **NOT PROMOTED but KEEP OPEN**. This is not a graveyard
result; it's a "real but marginal, deserves a deeper probe" result.

Result.md: `runs/746_value_oracle/result.md`.

already non-zero regardless of where 746 lands on its next probe.
