## Major Accomplishments

### 1. Dojo v2 Implementation (TASK_045)

Created complete curriculum training manager: `tools/orchestration/dojo_v2.py`

**Three-Phase Training Per Level:**
- Phase A: 300 epochs (basic, no noise, no fees)
- Phase B: 250 epochs (+ synthetic-noise 0.3)
- Phase C: 250 epochs (+ noise + fees 16bps + slip 5bps)
- Total: 800 epochs per level

**15 Levels (0-14):**
| Levels | Category | Patterns |
|--------|----------|----------|
| 0-4 | Basic Mechanics | sine, lagged, dip, noisy_trends, rotation |
| 5-7 | Statistical Arbitrage | pairs_trading, triangle_arb, index_arb |
| 8-10 | Momentum & Reversal | momentum, mean_reversion, breakout |
| 11-13 | Microstructure | bid_ask_bounce, order_imbalance, market_making |
| 14 | Volatility | volatility_clustering (GARCH) |

**Kitchen Sink v2 Production Config:**
- PopArt + GAE
- Adaptive entropy (0.55 threshold)
- Per-head gradient clipping
- ReDo (dormant neuron recycling)
- ATC (contrastive learning)
- MCTS warmup (4→24 sims over 300 epochs)
- Physics-informed prediction loss
