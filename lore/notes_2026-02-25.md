### The 12% Oracle Support Observation

At 88% AR, only 12% of episodes use oracle state channels — yet the model achieves 60-80% balanced accuracy (seesaw between train and val). This demonstrates:
- The model is nearly self-sufficient (88% of episodes use its own predictions for state)
- The remaining 12% oracle support acts as a stabilizer, preventing full turtle collapse
- Removing these last training wheels (88% → 100% AR) is the exponentially hard part

**Best agreement reached: 74.4%** (E285 checkpoint) — 0.6% from 75% target. The model capability is clearly there; now it's about AR independence.

### Trade Confidence as a Measurable Metric

The logit diagnostic from merged_0009 (E184) provides a quantifiable metric for trade readiness:
- **Mean trade probability: ~22%** (avg of sell 20% + buy 24%)
- **Mean HOLD probability: 56.2%**
- **HOLD margin: 34.2%** (how much HOLD beats trade actions)
- **Max non-HOLD prob: 49.3%** (closest any trade action came to winning)

This is trackable across checkpoints. As training progresses:
- HOLD margin should shrink (56% → 50% → below 50% for some assets)
- Max non-HOLD prob should cross 50% (model starts trading on real data)
- Trade probability distribution should widen (different assets get different confidence)

**Re-run the logit diagnostic at each merge milestone** to track HOLD margin compression.

### Multi-GPU Scaling Theory (Not Implementing)

Discussed hypothetical 4-GPU training strategies:

1. **Data parallel (pmap)**: Split batch across GPUs, all-reduce gradients. Linear speedup on training but doesn't help the AR bottleneck.
2. **Local SGD**: Independent epochs + frequent merge. Already doing this with e-dojo at 20-epoch intervals.
3. **Hybrid (best for this workload)**: 2 GPUs generate AR rollout episodes in parallel + 2 GPUs train. Pipeline generation and training.

AR rollout is the bottleneck (90s→500s = 410s of rollout per epoch). Multi-GPU wins come from parallelizing generation, not gradient compute.

### AR Rollout Compression Options (Theoretical, Not Implementing)

Ranked by effort-vs-payoff:
1. **Verify detached rollout** (stop_gradient) — free, verify it's there
2. **Chunked rollout** (8-16 step chunks, frozen state within) — biggest win ~4-8x
3. **Sparse rollout** (recompute every N steps, interpolate) — modest gain
4. **Cached delta** (only recompute where actions changed) — clever but fragile
5. **Reduced precision rollout** (bfloat16) — trivial, small gain
6. **State prediction head** — research project, risky

Not implementing — diminishing returns vs bug risk at this stage. 500s/epoch is livable within the 2500-epoch budget.

### Architecture: Intelligence-Dominant MEV

The core thesis: most MEV bots are reactive state machines (event → rule → action). The Apothecary stack provides a **predictive planning agent** (history → regime model → opportunity forecast → multi-step search → optimal bundle).

**TRM inference latency**: 1-5ms forward pass, 50-300ms with MCTS — well within MEV's 500ms budget. The 1-minute candle is training data granularity, not inference ceiling.

**Strategy tiers** (by fit to our system):

| Tier | Strategy | Fit | Edge |
|------|----------|-----|------|
| 1A | Predictive liquidation positioning | Strong | Bella regime prediction → minutes of advance preparation |
| 1B | Cyclic arb (4+ hops) | Strong | MCTS explores 10^14 path space where greedy fails |
| 1C | Regime-aware MEV density | Strong | Adaptive compute allocation — concentrate firepower during volatile periods |
| 1D | CEX-DEX temporal arb | Strong | Kraken data + DEX data = lead-lag model (requires inventory capital) |
| 2A | Backrun optimization | Moderate | Bella impact prediction + MCTS multi-venue routing |
| 2B | JIT liquidity (enhanced) | Moderate | Swap size prediction + conditional execution |
| 3 | Simple sandwich / 2-pool arb | Skip | Zero intelligence edge, commoditized, ethically questionable |

### Foxglove Curriculum for MEV (L0-L14)

Proposed MEV-specific curriculum mapping the same Expert Iteration pattern:
- L0-L5: Basic detection and sizing (arb, liquidation, gas-aware profitability)
- L6-L10: Competition modeling, regime-conditional strategy, cross-protocol compound opportunities

The "expert" for MEV training is **historical hindsight** — with the full archive node, we can reconstruct every block's optimal MEV bundle offline (brute force OK when not real-time).
