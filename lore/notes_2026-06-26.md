Trajectory over 1498 epochs (10 bins):

| epoch | trade% | policy-ent | visit-ent | value-loss | alpha% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–150 | 7.7 | 3.99 | 2.87 | 1.21 | −1.85 |
| 601–750 | 5.9 | 3.00 | 2.13 | 1.05 | −2.15 |

| 1351–1498 | 4.9 | 2.59 | 2.00 | 1.15 | **−2.42** |

**Not an over-trade death-spiral — the opposite.** The policy went to **~95% HOLD almost immediately** (only
7.7% trading even at the start) and parked there. On a **post-cost objective over a cost-bound signal, the RL
correctly discovered that trading loses money net of 21 bp/side**, so it learned to barely trade. The
mitigations worked as designed — entropy fell (3.99→2.59) then **plateaued** (ε-greedy + entropy bonus
*contained* the collapse; it never railed to 0). But **containment ≠ convergence**: in-sample alpha sat at
~−2% the entire run and was *worse* at the end. Everything has been flat since ~epoch 1050.

This is precisely the outcome the operator pre-named: **"spiral-contained-but-no-learning = fail, the
definitive RL close."** The **planning side reproduces the cost-floor verdict** the linear/810 side found —
from the opposite direction. There is no profitable policy for the planner to find, so it learns the only
rational thing under an honest post-cost objective: **don't trade.**
