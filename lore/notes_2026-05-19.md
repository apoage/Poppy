## §141 — Why this matters methodologically

This is the **second time the campaign has been bitten by variant-only
extension framing**, after 734 went 3-seed +3.38pp → 5-seed +0.20pp.
But it's a different failure mode this time:

- **734 reversal** was a *seed-noise reversal*: s42 was an outlier
  (−12.71pp Δα), so the additional seed revealed wider variance than
  the 3-seed sample suggested. The methodology lesson was "extend to
  5-seed before declaring."

- **746 revision** is a *pairing-vs-proxy correction*: the variant
  was fine; the assumption that baselines would land at the 3-seed
  mean was wrong. The methodology lesson is "variant-only extension
  has an implicit pairing assumption that doesn't always hold —
  invest in proper paired baselines before declaring 5-seed."

The two together compound: even if 746 had passed both tests, the
+0.72pp signal is well within the documented seed-noise floor (~5pp
single-seed). Marginal mechanisms at this scale need 7+ seed
confirmation or stacking to climb out of the noise band.

**Updated 5-seed protocol** (load-bearing for future SPECs):
- 3-seed = screening (not promotion-eligible regardless of how clean)
- 5-seed paired = promotion test (mean ≥ +3pp AND no seed < −5pp)
- Proxy-paired (variant-only extension against baseline mean) =
  "preliminary 5-seed" — produces a *direction* signal but cannot
  be promotion-eligible. Always commission paired baselines before
  declaring.
- If proxy-paired shows promise but paired hasn't run yet: prepare
  the paired baselines as part of the same wave, not as a follow-up
  ("proxy-paired" is now considered incomplete, not "ready for
  promotion review").

This is now policy. Update SPEC templates accordingly when next one
is authored.
