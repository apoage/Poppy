### What the §170 entry didn't anticipate

The §170 close called Phase-2 load contract "more permissive than feared". That was correct for the load contract — `train_phase2.load_phase1_checkpoint` ate the bridge ckpt without complaint. But the **buffer init path** in `train_easy.py` had no equivalent permissiveness; canon prefill was the default and the bridge flag did not redirect it. The Phase-A smoke didn't catch this because the smoke uses a synthetic mini-buffer, not the full prefill path that fires on first real Phase-A start.

Lesson to carry forward: "ships in a day" is fine when the spec is complete and the load contract is permissive, but the **buffer state machine** is its own integration surface separate from "does the trainer eat the checkpoint". Both have to be covered by smoke before claiming Phase-A is unblocked.
