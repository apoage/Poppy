### Architectural Note: Limit Orders Are MuZero Actions

LimitSell/LimitBuy at 0.002% is **expected, not a bug**. The oracle has perfect hindsight — it never needs to express uncertainty via limit orders. Limit orders are fundamentally about hedging directional uncertainty, which only arises in the MCTS search tree (Poppy phase). When the search is split between bull/bear branches, a limit order is the natural hedge.

**Implication:** Foxglove's 5-action space effectively operates as 3 actions (Sell/Hold/Buy). LimitSell/LimitBuy activate in Poppy when MCTS provides the uncertainty signal. No fix needed at Foxglove level.
