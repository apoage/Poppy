[[project_oracle_design_intent]] (NEW). Two read-only forensic agents dispatched; verdicts:

- **H1 (differential reward):** the base *training* reward is ABSOLUTE — symlog log-return of portfolio
  value (`mcts_trader/env/jax_trading_env.py:1210–1214`); `differential_reward` exists but is flag-gated
  default-OFF (`train_jax_fast.py:1028`). BUT alpha = agent − market is the **scorecard everywhere**, so
  model selection has always optimized beat-the-market, and differential was the named confound of the
  06-28 coarse-bar test. Where the differential term *is* active, three lines of regime arithmetic
  (short-in-bear ≈ +2|m|, long-in-bull ≈ 0, hold-in-bull −m) mechanically produce the observed short
  bias. The bias is in the objective's geometry, not the learner.
- **H2 (oracle):** the oracle is direction-symmetric (path_solver ranks |forward return|) and scores
  **+265% in the bull window** — bull blindness is NOT oracle asymmetry. It's HOLD class imbalance
  (k=4 of 64 assets → **93.75% HOLD labels**) + foresight-teacher contamination. The two
  decontamination specs — TASK_775 oracle-mask, TASK_784 smoothed-oracle — were **authorized and never
  run** (784 dropped as "proven futile by proxy"). [[project_bull_blindness]] updated.

  multi-regime × oracle-as-eval-only — was decided 2026-06-27 and **never run**; the campaign pivoted
  to 819 the next morning. Every "MCTS cannot trade" datapoint carries at least one contamination.
- **Number provenance:** there is no clean "20k%" artifact — it's a memory of a family of bug-inflated
  figures (the 50,000% oracle_α was a k=4 **summed-not-averaged** 4× leverage bug; honest instrument
  readings: 65–136% per 400-bar episode at k=4/cost=0, +754%/ep net for the selective 42bp zigzag).
