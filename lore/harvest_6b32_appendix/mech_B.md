## 5. SCRR (Shock-Cohort Residual Reversal) — known ONLY via its REFUTER (`result_aa5a6d855b93d6383.json`): **REFUTED** (designer report did not survive; refuter's summary used)

Honest round trip with shock-day spreads: 82–182 bp → κ 0.10 nets +0.43..−0.57% per position; positivity alone needs harvestable κ ≥ 0.15–0.20.
