### Math for why gate doesn't move at default `-5` init

- sigmoid(-5) ≈ 0.0067, sigmoid'(-5) ≈ 0.0067
- gradient on gate logit measured at ~0.012
- per-step movement = lr × grad = 3e-5 × 0.012 = 3.6e-7
- 50 smoke steps × 3.6e-7 = ~1.8e-5 (observed -1e-6, same order)
- 200-epoch full run × 50 steps/epoch = 10,000 steps × 3.6e-7 = 3.6e-3

- 50 smoke steps × 2.4e-6 = ~1.2e-4 (10× the previous), should clear the 1e-3 criterion at 200 epochs
