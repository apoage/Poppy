## TASK_809 — decision-rule rescue → in flight; the *rule* was the failure, not the signal

Thesis (operator): 808 died on the **decision rule** (a clock that trades every bar regardless of

### The smoke earned its keep — three design flaws caught before any full run

This is the lesson worth keeping: each re-smoke caught a real flaw that would have produced a
plausible-but-wrong result.

- **v1 — inert hysteresis.** Modeled hysteresis as a band on the weight *change* `|Δw|≤h/k`. But a
  top-k book has **binary** weights (0 or 1/k), so Δw is always ±1/k — the band never bites. Smoke
  turnover stayed ~1.0, trade%=100. The acceptance check ("if turnover doesn't drop, the gate isn't
  working") flagged it.
- **v2 — maker fiction.** Switched Arm A to a rank-buffer (worked: turnover → 0.56) but modeled maker
  as a **rebate** (−2/−5 bps) — the wrong regime. Net negative cost → the strategy *minted money*
  (+939 Sharpe, +5789% PnL). Operator correction: **Kraken maker is a POSITIVE fee (~0–16 bps), not a
  rebate — it halves the cost vs taker, never negates it.** The free corner is fiction and must be
  flagged + excluded.
- **v3 — conviction-percentile + min-hold (the real fix).** τ rescaled from an (inert) absolute
  `Σ|pred|>cost` gate to a **conviction percentile** (hold only the top-(1−τ) fraction by pred —
  relative conviction, because IC 0.15 ranks well but its *magnitudes* are noisy/inflated, which is
  exactly why the absolute gate never bit). Plus a **min-hold N** (a name stays ≥N bars once entered —
  the direct churn cap; operator's complementary lever, and the one that actually worked).

### v3 re-smoke result (H=5, 5-day window) — both hard checks pass; the tension is the story

- **Check 1 (turnover ≪ 1.0): PASSES.** Min-hold N=60 → turnover **0.033 (LO) / 0.086 (LS)** vs 808's

- **Check 2 (maker honest): PASSES.** Realistic cells (fee/adv ≥ 10bp) lose hard; free corner flagged

  - **long-short N=60: gross stays positive** (+0.37 PnL free) — market-neutral survives staleness

- **Verdict (smoke): 0/32 realistic.** But the LS corner is **marginal, not dead**: gross +0.37 vs
  cumulative cost ~0.31 at the **5bp net-maker** point (Kraken high-volume fee≈0 + adv≈5) → ~+0.05.
  The smoke's coarse cost grid (0/10bp) *brackets* 5bp without testing it, and only ran H=5.

The recurring enemy this turn was **systemd-oomd pressure-kills**, not job size. gpu-server ran
memory-contended (6–15 G free, ~380 G reclaimable page cache, co-tenants ComfyUI 20 G + VBox 10 G +

the cap.**

The proper fix (operator: "don't launch 57 G against 15 G free and hope the ulimit catches it — that's
watcher-reliance"): **chunk the H=1 fit.** Streaming normal-equations ridge — accumulate `ZᵀZ` + `ZᵀY`
in 20k-row blocks, solve once; value-identical to the one-shot solve by construction, gated by the
inherited-IC check (== 806 within 1e-3). **Sizecheck confirmed BOTH gates: chunked H=1 fit peak =
4.22 GB** (down from ~57 G, ~12 min) **and IC value-identity = +0.151053 vs 806's 0.1511 (MATCH, |Δ|=
5.3e-5).** Both pre-launch conditions met; the full 809 run launched under the swap-capped scope.

## Lessons (Bloom-worthy)

1. **The smoke is a design-flaw catcher, not a formality.** Three iterations, three real flaws caught
   before burning a 30-min run or misleading the auditor — inert-hysteresis, maker-rebate-fiction,
   τ-scale. Cheap smokes with *hard mechanical checks* (turnover must drop; nothing may mint) earn their
   keep.
2. **Predicted magnitude ≠ realized edge.** IC measures *rank* correlation; a "trade when predicted edge
   > cost" gate never bites because predicted magnitudes are inflated. Use *relative* conviction
   (percentile), not absolute thresholds, on rank-quality signals.
3. **Low turnover ⟺ stale book ⟺ decayed edge** is the central tension for a fast-decaying 1-min signal.
   Min-hold buys low turnover but spends edge; the question is whether any (τ,N) leaves enough.
4. **OOM-safety is a construction problem, not a watcher problem.** Under host memory pressure, oomd
   kills on PSI regardless of your cgroup cap. The answer is to *not need the memory* (chunked
   normal-equations, peak 57 G → 4.22 G), verified value-identical, not to tune kill-thresholds.
