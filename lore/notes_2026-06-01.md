### A2 — put the *realized* return in the loss
If "penalty too weak" is the diagnosis, the principled fix isn't a bigger penalty — it's a gradient
that points at money. **A2 (TASK_790)** adds a realized-PnL value term to Stage-B: read the expected
per-asset position straight off the policy (`P(Buy)+P(LimitBuy) − P(Sell) − P(LimitSell)` ∈ [−1,1]),
multiply by the **next-step realized log-return** (`future_log_returns[:,0]`, already a JIT input),
and MSE it onto the best-achievable `|r_next|`. That pushes `pos → sign(r_next)` scaled by move size
— a direct "be on the right side of the next bar" gradient, with no oracle hindsight and no new param
leaf. Loss-side only, compile-time gated (`realized_value_weight` default 0 → bit-identical OFF).

Built by hand, not by agent: both build sub-agents came back **write-blocked at the harness level**
(Edit/Write/Bash denied in their worktrees), so they returned designs and I applied the diff myself.
Worth noting as a recurring failure mode of the worktree-isolated builders this week.

The watch on A2 is the same anti-correlation tripwire: does Δα move **without** a trade-rate
explosion? That's the real test of whether PnL-in-loss is the unlock or just another way to overtrade.
