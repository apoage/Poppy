## §211 — The dynamics verdict: TASK_804 branch C is NULL on both surfaces — the pre-registered structural bet dies clean

**What was on the bench.** Coming into today the perception side was already flat (§209): `real_fe`
failed to beat its own cyclic-shift control, `shift_fe` quietly edging it everywhere. But §210 was
emphatic that the front-end null retires nothing — branch C's actual claim was always that token value, if
it exists, lives on the **planning side**, in the token-grounded dynamics head (TASK_802's NextTokenHead,
the standing hypothesis since TASK_761). Arms 4–5 — `real_dyn` vs `shift_dyn` — were the test that counts,
and the TASK_804 promote rule was written in advance: **arm4−arm1 mean Δα ≥ +3pp AND arm4 > arm5**.
Anything less and the candle-language bet nulls on the surface it was actually built for.

**What happened.** The dynamics runs completed off `/tmp/dyn_solo.py` (GPU 2 train/eval, seed-major
order, the same per-run frozen-log watchdog + systemd-scope reap as the front-end runner). Final
eval-MCTS sims=16 `avg_alpha`, all five arms, read from `runs/804_token_arms/eval_*/benchmark.json`
(`summary.avg_alpha`):

| arm | s7 | s13 | s23 | mean | Δα vs raw |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|------|-----------|
| raw (canon a1b1)           | 0.0674 | 0.0573 | 0.0671 | 0.0640 | — |
| real_fe (perception)       | 0.0641 | 0.0558 | 0.0684 | 0.0628 | −0.12pp |
| shift_fe (perception null) | 0.0768 | 0.0605 | 0.0704 | 0.0692 | +0.53pp |
| real_dyn (planning)        | 0.0641 | 0.0655 | 0.0658 | 0.0651 | +0.12pp |
| shift_dyn (planning null)  | 0.0708 | 0.0580 | (unrecoverable) | — | — |

`real_dyn` lands at **+0.12pp** vs raw. The promote bar is **+3pp**. It misses by an order of magnitude —
**NO PROMOTE**. Both real arms sit essentially on top of canon (real_fe −0.12pp, real_dyn +0.12pp) while
the shuffled controls do as well or better (shift_fe +0.53pp). All five arms cluster in a ~2pp band
(0.057–0.077). This is precisely the "arms 2≈3≈4≈5≈1" outcome the spec named for the all-null case.

**The confirmation, named.** This is not a reversal — it confirms, on the surface that matters, the prior
that has held since TASK_738: every classifier/encoder/perception/planning-perturbation mechanism on this
stack comes back null-or-worse on deployment Alpha
([[project_single_mechanism_exhausted.md]]). The dynamics arm was the candle-language program's one
genuinely untested claim — the planning-side bet that §209/§210 protected from premature burial — and it
joins the rest. Branch C nulls on both perception and planning. The disciplined read: tokenisation does
not move the trader.

**Lesson.** A structural bet is only worth running when you have written the decision rule before the
data lands. `real_dyn`'s +0.12pp against a pre-registered +3pp bar is a *clean* no — there is no posthoc
threshold to argue about, no "but if you squint" because the squinting was forbidden in advance. The
front-end null cleared the gate; the dynamics null resolved the bet. Running arms 4–5 rather than writing
the obituary at arms 1–3 is exactly what makes this negative publishable instead of an unfinished story.

## §212 — The 0.24pp flutter we refuse to chase, and the cursed cell we accepted

**The honest flutter — recorded precisely so it isn't over- or under-read.** There is one place the
"token grounding helps planning" hypothesis showed its face. `real_dyn` (+0.12pp) edges `real_fe`
(−0.12pp) by ~0.24pp — the only direction in which the dynamics head outperforms the front-end. And in a
per-seed control comparison, seed-13 `real_dyn` beat its own `shift_dyn` control by +0.75pp (0.0655 >
0.0580). Read alone, that is the shape of the hoped-for signal: aligned token content carrying value into
the planning head.

But it does not survive its neighbours. Seed-7 `shift_dyn` *won* its pair (0.0708 > real_dyn 0.0641); the
seed-23 control never landed (see below). Per-seed `real_dyn` Δα vs raw runs s7 −0.33pp, s13 +0.82pp,
s23 −0.14pp → a mean of +0.12pp riding on a **1.2pp seed-to-seed swing**. The entire spread sits inside
the project's own standing rule that single-seed Alpha gaps ≤ ~5pp are noise. A 0.24pp mean edge with a
1.2pp inter-seed swing is not a signal; it is what noise looks like when it happens to lean one way. The
disciplined verdict is **null-with-a-flutter**, not **weak-positive** — and the discipline of saying so is
the second Bloom beat here. The faint asymmetry is real and recorded; chasing it with more seeds would be
spending the most expensive resource on the residue of a no.

**The cursed cell — a real operational scar.** `shift_dyn_seed23` never completed. It failed **8 times
total**: 3 attempts on the 2-wide partition runner (all frozen-log wedges, ~20min each) and 5 on a
dedicated SOLO retry runner (wedges plus one clean exit at epoch 0 before any training began). This is the
[[project_seed7_stageb_wedge]] class at its worst — and notably *not* seed-7-specific and *not*
2-wide-specific (it wedged solo too), but concentrated on the seed-23 dynamics cells:
`real_dyn_seed23` also wedged 3× before a retry runner finally caught it. The per-run frozen-log watchdog
+ systemd-scope reap did its job every single time — no orphans, no GPU leak — but the underlying Stage-B
hang remains **undiagnosed** and is now the single biggest reliability risk for any future paired campaign
on this stack. We accepted 14/15 because the missing cell only completes the seed-23 real-vs-shift
*direction*; it cannot move the no-promote decision, which is locked by `real_dyn`'s +0.12pp mean. The
honest scorecard is 14 cells, one acknowledged hole, and a decision that does not depend on the hole.

**What this closes, and what stands.** Branch C — the candle-language tokenisation bet (TASK_789/794/
800–804) — is **closed as a trader mechanism**: it moves Alpha on neither perception nor planning. What
survives is the measurement infrastructure, and it is worth keeping: the L1 BPE tokenizer plus the
grammar/lag certification (TASK_799/800); the **cyclic-shift null methodology** — a clean,
content-preserving control that destroys only alignment, reusable for any future "does aligned X carry
signal" question; and the token-grounded dynamics head (TASK_802) as a now-validated-but-null
architectural option. The strategic frame from §209 holds and is now complete: the front-end null was
infrastructure-clearing, the planning null is the bet resolving, and conflating the two was the trap we
avoided — avoiding it is exactly why we ran arms 4–5 rather than writing the epitaph at arms 1–3.

**Operational close-out.** All 15 runs are off the GPUs; gpu-server is **idle** as of the verdict. The
campaign ran two self-advancing solo runners + a partitioned 2-wide pass + three single-cell retry runners
across ~30h, surviving 4 infra blockers at launch and ~14 sporadic Stage-B wedges, all absorbed by per-run
watchdogs. The deploy contract held throughout — surgical scps, never a full deploy mid-campaign, and
sub-agents never deployed.

**Lesson.** The publishable shape of a dead branch is: a pre-registered decision rule, a clean null on the
surface that mattered, a faint asymmetry named and *declined*, and an honest accounting of the one cell
that wouldn't run. Branch C ends not with a failure to measure but with a measurement that came back no —
and the apparatus that produced it is the part worth carrying forward.
