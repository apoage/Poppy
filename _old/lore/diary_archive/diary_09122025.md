# Diary Entry - 2025-12-09

## Summary of Changes Implemented:

1.  **Resolved "NaN Loss":** Confirmed that using `int32` for saving values in the JAX pipeline resolved the issue.
2.  **Fixed Paper Trading Validation:**
    *   Implemented a dedicated `ActionLogger` in `paper_trade_jax.py` that records every trade (timestamp, symbol, action, price, size, fee, model confidence, portfolio value, cash balance) to `trade_log.csv`. This fulfills the request for saving all executed commands for validation and investigation.
3.  **Realistic Market Simulation:**
    *   The `PortfolioManager` (`mcts_trader/agent/portfolio.py`) was significantly refactored to incorporate realistic financial mechanics:
        *   **Trading Fees:** Implemented deduction of fees (default 0.26% taker) from cash for every buy and sell order.
        *   **Slippage:** Introduced slippage (default 0.05%) by adjusting execution prices (buying slightly higher, selling slightly lower).
        *   **Correct Equity Calculation:** The `update_prices` method now accurately calculates total portfolio equity by subtracting short liabilities from cash and long asset values.
4.  **Training Integration:** The `FastTradingEnv` used in `train_jax_fast.py` was updated to initialize `PortfolioManager` with these new realistic fee and slippage parameters (26 bps fee, 5 bps slippage). This ensures the agent is trained under conditions that reflect real-world costs.
5.  **Inference Integration:** The `PaperTrader` in `paper_trade_jax.py` was updated to initialize `PortfolioManager` with the same realistic fee and slippage parameters, ensuring consistency between training and paper trading environments.
6.  **Standard Environment Update:** The generic `TradingEnv` in `mcts_trader/env/trading_env.py` was also updated to accept and use `trading_fee_bps` and `slippage_bps` parameters, promoting consistency across any environment instances.
7.  **Verification:** Comprehensive unit tests (`test_portfolio_math.py`) were created and executed to verify the mathematical correctness of fee deduction, slippage application, and the updated equity calculation, all of which passed. The temporary test file was subsequently removed.

These changes provide a more robust and realistic simulation environment for both training and evaluating the JAX MuZero trading agent. The paper trading session was then initiated to observe the model's performance in this enhanced environment.
