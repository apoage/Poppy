## Executive Summary

Your "dual collapse" phenomenon—where policy entropy collapses to HOLD actions while the auxiliary prediction head learns trivial "no-change" solutions—is a recognized failure mode in model-based RL. This represents a **self-reinforcing feedback loop**: policy collapse → impoverished training data → prediction collapse → useless gradients → accelerated policy collapse. The literature identifies this as a combination of **distribution shift**, **plasticity loss**, and **exploration failure** in jointly-trained systems.

---

## 1. How MuZero/AlphaZero Prevent Policy Collapse

### Core Mechanisms

**AlphaZero/MuZero implementations rely on several key safeguards:**

1. **Dirichlet Noise Injection**: At the root of MCTS, noise is added to the prior policy to ensure all actions have non-zero probability, preventing premature convergence. The formula from AlphaGo Zero uses: `P(s,a) = (1 - ε)p_a + εη_a` where η_a ~ Dirichlet(α) .

2. **Temperature Scheduling**: The visit count distribution is raised to temperature τ: `π_a ∝ N(s,a)^(1/τ)`. During training, τ starts high (exploration) and anneals, but never reaches zero. Production systems like AlphaStar maintain τ > 0.5 throughout training.

3. **Reanalyse Mechanism**: MuZero regenerates fresh targets from old trajectories by re-running MCTS with the *latest* network, providing continuous novelty and preventing stale data from reinforcing collapsed policies , .

4. **Policy Prior Regularization**: The policy network serves as a prior that regularizes MCTS toward areas where the learned model is accurate, preventing overfitting to model errors .

### Critical Implementation Details

From Reddit discussions on MuZero collapse :
- **Learning rate warm-up** is essential—cold-starting with full LR causes immediate collapse
- **Gradient clipping** at 0.5-1.0 prevents value head gradients from dominating policy updates
- **Experience replay prioritization** must be tuned to prioritize diverse state-action pairs

## 2. Causes of Auxiliary Prediction Head Collapse

### Primary Mechanisms

**The prediction head learns trivial solutions due to:**

1. **Data Distribution Collapse**: When the policy emits HOLD (action=0) 90%+ of the time, the dynamics model only sees flat trajectories. The optimal solution becomes predicting "no change" (MSE minimization incentivizes this) .

2. **Gradient Starvation**: The physics-informed ODE component produces small gradients when trajectories are static. The neural corrector then dominates with a trivial identity mapping.

3. **Loss Weight Imbalance**: In MuZero's joint loss `L_total = L_value + L_policy + L_reward + L_auxiliary`, if `L_auxiliary` is weighted too high early in training, it can overpower policy exploration signals.

### Evidence from Literature

**Dreamer-VXS** shows that auxiliary predictions (reward/discount) are "crucial for policy learning in imagination" but require careful weighting: `L_model = L_VAE + L_RSSM + λ_rL_r + λ_dL_d` . Poor λ tuning causes the RSSM state to collapse.

**ReCoRe** identifies that contrastive learning in world models causes encoder collapse when "policy learning is disconnected from world-model learning" . Their solution: intervention-invariant auxiliary tasks (depth reconstruction) that remain valid under augmentations.

## 3. Entropy Regularization Weighting

### Recommended Practices

**Relative Weighting:**
- **Policy loss coefficient**: 1.0 (baseline)
- **Entropy regularization**: 0.01-0.05 relative to policy loss
- **Value loss**: 0.25-0.5 relative to policy loss
- **Auxiliary prediction loss**: 0.1-0.2 relative to policy loss (during warmup)

**Dynamic Scheduling:**
```python
# EfficientZero V2 uses curriculum scheduling
entropy_coef = initial_coef * (1 - progress) + final_coef * progress
# where progress ∈ [0, 1] and final_coef ≈ 0.001
```

**Temperature Annealing:**
- Early training: τ = 1.0 (uniform MCTS exploration)
- Mid training: τ = 0.25-0.5 (softmax policy)
- Late training: τ = 0.1-0.25 (sharpening but not collapsing)

### Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Static entropy coefficients**: Reddit reports  show this leads to inevitable collapse
- **Adaptive entropy boosting** (increasing coef when entropy drops) provides only temporary relief—the root cause remains
- **Overweighting auxiliary loss** (>0.5) during early training accelerates prediction collapse

## 4. Decoupling Policy and World Model Training

### Proven Techniques

**1. DMO (Decoupled Forward-Backward Model-Based Optimization) , :**
- **Forward pass**: Use high-fidelity simulator for trajectory generation
- **Backward pass**: Use learned differentiable model for gradient computation
- **Benefit**: Prevents compounding errors while maintaining analytical gradients

**2. Alternating Training Schedules:**
```python
# RL-AUX framework 
for epoch in range(total_epochs):
    # Phase 1: Train auxiliary head on frozen policy data
    train_auxiliary_only()
    # Phase 2: Train policy with fixed auxiliary predictions
    train_policy_only()
```
This breaks the feedback loop by ensuring each component sees diverse data.

**3. Conservative Planning :**
MuZero's policy prior biases MCTS toward regions where the model is accurate. Decouple this by:
- Training world model on policy's behavior distribution
- Training policy on imagined rollouts from a separate exploration policy

**4. Reset-Based Interventions :**
- Periodically reinitialize auxiliary head weights (every 50k steps)
- Use "Shrink and Perturb" : `w ← 0.99w + 0.01N(0,σ²)`

## 5. MCTS Simulation Count vs. Policy Entropy

### Empirical Relationship

**Low simulation counts (<50):**
- High variance in visit distributions
- Entropy remains artificially high due to random tie-breaking
- Poor policy improvement

**Moderate counts (50-200):**
- Optimal entropy range (3.5-4.5 for 320 actions)
- MCTS statistically significant exploration
- Balance between exploitation and noise

**High counts (>500):**
- **Entropy collapse risk**: Visit distribution concentrates on single action
- Policy prior dominates, Dirichlet noise becomes negligible
- Trading environments particularly sensitive due to sparse rewards

### Recommended Configuration

For 320-action trading domain:
- **Training**: 100-150 simulations with τ = 0.5-1.0
- **Inference**: 500-800 simulations with τ = 0.1
- **Dirichlet noise**: α = 0.15, ε = 0.25 (higher than board games due to action space size)

**Critical Insight**: Increasing simulations without temperature scheduling accelerates collapse. Use **dynamic rollout allocation** : increase simulations proportionally to iteration number, but maintain temperature.

## 6. Exploration Bonuses and Value Function Interaction

### Intrinsic Motivation Integration

**Novelty-based bonuses** , :
```python
# Pseudocount-based exploration
bonus(s) = β / √(visit_count(s) + 1)
# Add to UCB: UCB(s,a) = Q(s,a) + c·P(s,a)·√(N(s))/(1+N(s,a)) + bonus(s)
```

**State visitation density models** :
- Train neural density model to predict state visitation
- Reward model for visiting low-probability states
- Prevents the "flat trajectory" trap in trading

### Interaction Effects

**Positive interactions**:
- Intrinsic bonuses maintain high stable rank 
- Dormant neurons stay active when exploring novel states 
- Continual backpropagation  maintains plasticity

**Negative interactions**:
- High exploration bonus → overfitting to bonus signal → value function misevaluation
- Must anneal β over time: β_t = β_0 × (1 - progress)^2

**Implementation**: In MuZero, add bonus to MCTS value estimate:
`v'(s) = v(s) + intrinsic_bonus(s)`

## 7. Failure Modes in World Models on Policy-Generated Data

### Documented Issues

**1. Value-Equivalent Model Limitations :**
- MuZero's model cannot evaluate policies far from data collection distribution
- Prediction errors compound exponentially for non-policy-compliant actions
- Policy collapse makes training data stationary, model learns narrow region

**2. Encoder-Decoder Mismatch in Dreamer :**
- Contrastive learning creates augmented views
- World model predicts original image from augmented latent
- **Encoder collapses** to trivial representation invariant to augmentations

**3. Dormant Neuron Phenomenon :**
- RL networks develop 40-60% inactive neurons by mid-training
- Gradient flow only through HOLD action pathway
- Auxiliary head's neural corrector receives no meaningful gradients

**4. Plasticity Loss , :**
- Standard Adam/Norm layers increase plasticity loss
- Weights grow large, network becomes "solidified"
- No capacity to learn new patterns when policy shifts

### Solutions from Literature

**ReDo (Recycling Dormant Neurons)** :
```python
# Reset neuron if inactive for >10k steps
if activation_moving_average < threshold:
    w, b ← reinitialize(w, b)
```

**Continual Backpropagation** :
- Reinitialize least-used units continuously
- Replacement rate: ~1 unit per 500 steps per layer

**Neuroplastic Expansion :**
- Start with small network, dynamically grow
- Prune dormant neurons, consolidate useful ones
- Outperforms fixed-size networks with 5x parameters

## 8. Production System Techniques

### AlphaGo/AlphaStar Implementation Details

**Population-Based Training (PBT)**:
- Maintain population of 10-20 agents
- Replace collapsed agents (entropy < 2.5) with copies of healthy ones
- Hyperparameter evolution: entropy_coef, LR, temperature

**Checkpoint Averaging**:
- AlphaGo Zero averaged past 20 checkpoints for evaluation
- Prevents overfitting to recent collapsed policy

**Exploration vs Exploitation in MCTS**:
- **First 30 moves**: τ=1.0, Dirichlet noise ε=0.25
- **Mid-game**: τ=0.5, ε=0.15  
- **Endgame**: τ=0.1, no noise
- Trading requires similar phases: market open/close vs mid-day

### OpenAI Five's Exploration Strategy

- **Intrinsic reward**: `r_intrinsic = 0.01 × teamfight_potential`
- **Forced diversity**: If entropy < 3.0, inject random actions for 100 steps
- **Separate behavior policy**: Collect data with more exploratory policy than learning policy

## Conclusion

The coupled collapse you're experiencing is fundamentally a **positive feedback loop** rooted in joint training dynamics. The literature consensus is that **decoupling + plasticity maintenance + entropy control** forms a robust solution. Start with immediate fixes (entropy floor, ReDo), then implement medium-term scheduling, and finally adopt architectural decoupling via DMO or Neuroplastic Expansion for production stability.
